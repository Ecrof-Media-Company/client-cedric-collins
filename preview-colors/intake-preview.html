Why the sensitive part stays off the website

A website form sends answers to an inbox. That is fine for a name and a phone number. It is not fine for Social Security numbers, license numbers, and children’s insurance policies. If an inbox is ever compromised, that information is exposed, and protecting it is part of your duty to keep client information confidential.
