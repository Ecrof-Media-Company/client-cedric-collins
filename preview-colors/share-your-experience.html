Thank you for trusting me with your family.

A few honest sentences from you help another parent in Central Ohio decide who to call on one of the hardest weeks of their life. It takes about three minutes. Here is what helps most.

Answer any two or three of these.

Where were you when you first called?How you were feeling, in general terms. Overwhelmed, unsure where to start, worried about your kids.
What did Cedric do that made a difference?How he explained things, how quickly he got back to you, how he prepared you for court.
What changed by the end?How you felt walking out. Clearer, calmer, back on your feet. You do not need to share the outcome.
Who would you recommend him to?For example, a parent going through a custody case or someone starting a divorce.

Please keep these private

  • Your children’s names, ages, or schools
  • The other party’s name
  • Case numbers, dollar amounts, or court details
  • Anything you would not want a neighbor to read

Your first name and last initial is plenty. Please write it in your own words. There is nothing to buy and nothing offered in return. An honest review is the whole point.
